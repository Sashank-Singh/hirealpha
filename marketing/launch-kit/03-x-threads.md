# 03 — X / Twitter: a week of threads

Format: mobile screenshots of real conversations **always beat text**. Each
thread = 1 screenshot + short build-up, or a short thread of 3–5 shots.

Posting rhythm: 1–2/day for a week, then 3–4/week forever. Reply to every
comment; the algorithm rewards engagement, and the product *is* the content.
