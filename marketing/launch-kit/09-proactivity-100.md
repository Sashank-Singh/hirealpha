# 100 proactive touches Alpha could make — scored like a human

What a caring older sibling or close friend actually does, day to day, without being asked. Not a feature list — a behavior list. Each scored 1–10 by (real value to the person × how often it would land well × how annoying it would be if wrong). Build order suggestion: start at the top of each domain.

Scoring: **10** = "I'm glad my person thought of that" almost every time. **5** = sometimes useful, sometimes noise. **1** = cute once, annoying weekly.

## The rule behind all 100
A person who cares does three things: **notices** (without being asked), **names it** (warmly, specifically), and offers **one small move** (never a lecture). Every touch above is that shape. The scores tell you where to start: the 9s and 10s (sleep-trend care, money catches, "you went quiet," remembering throwaway details, overwork intervention) are the ones that make someone tell a friend about Alpha.
