# 01 — Show HN

Post from a fresh-but-not-new HN account (a few weeks of normal comments first;
HN kills brand-new-account Show HNs). Title ≤ 80 chars.

## Title options

1. `Show HN: I hired an AI assistant that lives in my iMessage`
2. `Show HN: An AI assistant with its own phone number — texts you first, does the thing ($19/mo)`
3. `Show HN: Alpha, a personal secretary that lives in Messages (3 separate hire types)`

Rule of thumb: lead with the *thing* ("an AI assistant in iMessage"), not the
brand. Lead with the assistant job, not the friend framing.

## Body (copy-paste, then trim to taste)

> I hired an AI assistant that lives in the normal Messages app. It's a contact
> with its own phone number, its own memory, and it texts me first instead of
> waiting for me to open an app. Not one chatbot with modes — and not an "AI
> friend": it's a secretary. You hire it to run things, not to fix loneliness.
>
> What it does (live now): texts me first — morning and evening briefs, catches
> a spending overage before Friday, reminds me I owe someone a reply. It checked
> me into my flight and moved dinner when the flight slipped. It drafts replies
> inside the thread and refuses to send anything until I tap approve. It builds
> me small apps in the chat instead of making me shop the App Store.
>
> Why iMessage: I already live there. No new app to remember to open, no
> notification I will ignore. It saves a number in Contacts and it just... texts.
>
> What surprised me: the memory and the proactivity are what make it feel like a
> person, not a prompt. It remembers details from weeks ago and uses them at the
> right moment, unasked.
>
> Honesty: it is an assistant and a second pair of eyes, not a therapist and
> not a replacement for a real person. Drafts never send without my tap.
> More hire types (Coworker, Cofounder) are in the workshop; the assistant is
> live right now.
>
> Stack: small independent team, own infra, Stripe for billing. $19/mo
> (currently $5/mo for the first two months), 7-day free trial. The site is also
> agent-ready: `llms.txt`, `openapi.json`, docs, markdown per page — useful if
> you or your agents want to evaluate it programmatically.
>
> I would love your worst-case tests. Try to make it cross a line; I want the
> guardrails tested.

## After you post

- Reply to EVERY comment in the first 24h. It is the ranking signal.
- The budget line ("$5/mo first two months") and the honesty paragraph stop the
  two predictable complaints (pricing and vibes).
- Expect the "another chatbot" and "just an LLM wrapper" comments. Answer with
  the memory + separate-hires point, and the approve-before-send behavior.
- If anyone asks "how is this better than X?" say: "most of this category is a
  closed waitlist and a roadmap. Ours is live, and the difference is it's a
  contact that texts you first, not an app you open." Never trash a named
  competitor.
