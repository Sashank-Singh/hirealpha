# 00 — Facts, verified against the repo (use only these)

## The product
- Three hireable AI agents: **Friend**, **Coworker**, **Cofounder**. Each is a
  real contact with its own phone number, memory, and personality, living in the
  normal Messages app (iMessage on iPhone, SMS elsewhere).
- **Not a chatbot with modes.** Separate numbers, separate memories, separate
  tones. Friend does not see your pipeline; Cofounder does not nag about protein.
- Each hire remembers you across threads and texts first with one useful thing.
- Hires act through connected tools (Gmail, Calendar, Maps, and more) and always
  ask before sending or spending — drafts wait for your tap.

## What each hire does (for screenshots and demos)
- **Friend** (live now): morning + evening briefs, sleep/food/training/spending
  tracking, people you are overdue to text, gentle check-ins without nagging.
- **Coworker** (workshop): the one next thing, meeting prep + recap, email draft
  review ("approve and send"), scheduling with free-slot views, Linear triage.
- **Cofounder** (workshop): pipeline board, runway, decision ledger, hire-decision
  side-by-sides, investor updates.
