# 06 — 30-Day LinkedIn + X Content Calendar

Positioning: **Alpha is your assistant.** It is NOT an "AI friend." This is not
about loneliness. Alpha is a personal secretary that does everything for you:
reminds you, catches what you're doing wrong, tells you what to do, and
handles it — so you just live your life. Everything happens inside your texts,
including the apps (superpowers) Alpha builds you on demand — instead of the
App Store.

Builds on the "everywhere launch" system: repetition over reach, one repeatable
claim, one magic moment, customer stories as our "trusted voices," and a 30-day
cadence instead of a single launch event.

## The claim (say it everywhere, every post)

> **Alpha is your assistant. It lives in your texts, texts you first, does the
> thing — and only asks when it has to. You just exist.**

Alternates (rotate, same core):
- "Don't prompt. Just text. Alpha hands you the finished thing."
- "The App Store is 10,000 apps. Alpha builds you the one you need — in your
  texts."
- "It catches the thing you'd have missed. That's the whole job."

## The magic moment (show in 5 seconds, no sound)

Alpha texts you first with something real: flight check-in → boarding pass
lands in the thread. Or: it caught a subscription/refund and has the draft
waiting for your tap. Result visible immediately, no app opened.

## Word rules (do not break)

- Say **assistant / secretary / "does it for you."** Never "AI friend,"
  "companion," "loneliness," "therapist."
- Alpha is a *hire you made* — a contact with its own number who works for you.
- Coworker and Cofounder are "in the workshop" (coming soon) — only Friend-class
  Alpha is live. Do not imply the others are live.
- Drafts never send without approval — say it, it's the trust wedge.
- No competitor names in public. Contrast the category: "most of these are
  closed waitlists or apps you have to open; ours texts you."
