# 02 — Product Hunt

## Setup (30 min the day before)

- Account with a few days of history (Product Hunt flags fresh accounts).
- Product name: **HireAlpha** — tagline (≤ 60 chars):
  `Alpha, an AI assistant that lives in your texts — texts you first`
- URL: `https://hirealpha.chat` — tagline: the same one from the site.
  $19/mo, 7-day free trial, no ads. Category: **Artificial Intelligence**.
- First comment gets pinned. Write it before you launch and paste at 00:01 PT.

## Gather the assets

1. **Hero image / video:** the phone demo looping (inbox → thread → typing).
   A 20-second screen recording beats a static image. Top banner 1500×1000.
2. **3 gallery images**, real conversation screenshots (consented + redacted):
   - Alpha checking in a flight + moving dinner.
   - Alpha "invoice says 4,000 / PO says 3,500, draft ready, approve and it sends."
   - Alpha building a mini-app in the thread (the App Store replacement).
   File names `alpha-checkin.png`, `alpha-approve-send.png`, `alpha-miniapp.png`.
3. **Launch day only:** a founder note in the first comment, and discount text
   (the $5/mo promo is already live on all paid tiers).

## First comment (pinned) — copy-paste

> I built HireAlpha because I kept downloading AI apps and, a week later,
> forgetting they exist.
>
> So I reversed it: instead of another app, you **hire an assistant who lives in
> your existing Messages app**. One contact with its own number and memory.
>
> - Texts you first — morning/evening briefs, catches my spending before it
>   happens, reminds me who I owe a reply.
> - It checked me into my flight and moved dinner when the flight slipped.
> - It drafts replies inside the thread — "approve and it sends." Nothing goes
>   out without my tap.
> - It builds me small apps in the chat instead of making me shop the App Store.
>
> This isn't an "AI friend" and it isn't about loneliness. It's a secretary: you
> hire it to run things so you just get to exist.
>
> Honest boundaries: it is an assistant, not a therapist; drafts never send
> without approval; and the whole thing runs on a small independent team with
> your data never sold or ad-targeted.
>
> Alpha is live today — $19/mo, currently $5/mo for your first two months, with
> a 7-day free trial. I read every comment. What would you make Alpha do?

## Launch-day rhythm (west-coast friendly)

- 00:01 PT — product goes live, first comment pinned from your own account.
- Morning — post the launch to your small circle + the X thread from `03`.
- All day — answer every comment in under an hour; ask testers for worst-case
  tests. Product Hunt is a board game played by responsiveness.
- Evening — thank-you comment + a "what broke" update post.

## After the 24h window

- The "makers" reply to you — say hi back. Keep the product page updated with
  the changelog as Coworker/Cofounder ship.
