About HireAlpha

HireAlpha is a small product with a simple thesis: the best place for an AI assistant is not another app you have to remember to open — it is the message thread you already live in. So we built three of them, and each one is a contact you text.

Friend keeps your personal life on track: sleep, food, training, spending, and the people you owe a reply. Coworker runs your workday: your one next action, meeting prep, draft review, scheduling, and issue triage. Cofounder thinks about the company: pipeline, runway, decisions, and investor notes.

Why three, not one

Most assistants are a single chatbot with modes, which means one blurry memory and one tone. We think the roles in your life are genuinely different, so each persona has its own phone number, its own memory, and its own personality. Friend does not see your pipeline; Cofounder does not nag you about protein.

Pricing

Each persona is $19 a month. You can hire one, two, or all three. There is no free tier that reads your data, and there is no advertising.

HireAlpha is made by a small independent team in San Francisco. We build in public, we answer email, and we would rather ship three focused hires than one everything-bot.
