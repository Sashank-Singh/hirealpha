HireAlpha — hire Friend, Coworker, and Cofounder as AI contacts in iMessage

HireAlpha puts three separate AI personas in your real message threads. Friend keeps your life on track — sleep, food, training, spending, and the people you owe a reply. Coworker runs your workday — meetings, drafts, Linear triage, and standups. Cofounder thinks about the company — pipeline, runway, decisions, and investor notes. Each has its own phone number, its own memory, and its own personality. They are not one chatbot with modes; they are three hires you text like anyone else.

Each persona is $19 a month. Start from the waitlist, connect your Google account, and text them like a contact. No new app to learn — the whole product lives in iMessage.

What each persona does

  • Friend — morning and evening briefs, body tracking, people you should text back, and a weekly review.
  • Coworker — the one next thing to do, meeting prep, draft review, scheduling, and issue triage.
  • Cofounder — pipeline board, decision ledger, hire calls, and investor updates.
